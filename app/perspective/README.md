# Perspective portal

## Product boundary

Perspective compares how political and editorial lenses frame the same verified event. It owns sources, claims, analysis, editorial review, corrections, and publication history. It is not an automated opinion generator or a claim that truth always sits in the middle.

## Current front-end

- Illustrative story labelled as a product demonstration.
- Three accessible lens controls with distinct framing and key questions.
- A shared-fact-base panel and explicit editorial note.
- Trust model covering provenance, labelled interpretation, human review, and uncertainty.

## Planned backend contract

Published articles should expose a versioned fact base, source list, individual claims, three editorial lenses, uncertainty notes, author/reviewer identity, published date, and correction history. AI-produced drafts never publish without accountable review.
