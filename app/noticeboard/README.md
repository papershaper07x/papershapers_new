# Noticeboard portal

## Product boundary

Noticeboard is a local, time-sensitive directory for offers, services, classes, events, and community posts. It owns places, geographic areas, posts, expiry, verification, reports, and moderation. It is not a social feed and should not optimise for outrage or infinite engagement.

## Current front-end

- Responsive local hero and tactile notice-card system.
- Category filtering over clearly illustrative Bengaluru listings.
- Placeholder posting handoff that explains the backend boundary.
- Product principles for freshness, distance, and transparent business information.

## Planned backend contract

Posts contain a verified place or organiser, coarse area, category, title, body, contact action, start/end time, and automatic expiry. Ranking prioritises relevance, distance, and freshness. Publishing and reporting require abuse controls and an auditable moderation queue.
