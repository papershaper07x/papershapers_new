# Ads, sponsorships and revenue

## 1. State what exists

No third-party ad scripts, sponsored checkout or ad revenue are active. Configuring a real publisher ID enables ads.txt only; it does not approve the site or start ads. Marketplace ordinary posting/reservation requests are free and commission is zero. Study Plus is a separate paid product.

## 2. Obtain eligibility and approval

Choose commercial-eligible hosting. Complete actual publisher identity, payout/tax information, domain verification and approval. Review current [eligibility](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/9724/eligibility-requirements-for-adsense?hl=en-uk), [UGC responsibility](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1355699) and [placement policies](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1346295). No minimum article count or guaranteed approval is claimed.

## 3. Choose safe inventory

If approved, start with reviewed original public guides and limited safe public marketplace surfaces. Exclude dummy content, private communications, authentication, dashboards, review tools, answers/assessments, child-directed inventory and pending/rejected notices. Automated moderation does not make all UGC ad-safe. Provide a rapid ad-disable and content-removal process.

## 4. Avoid deceptive placement

Label each advertisement/sponsorship clearly. Keep it away from reserve/cancel, exam answers, navigation and download controls. Reserve layout space and test mobile reflow. No disguised buttons, overlays, fake urgency, click incentives or impression-generating auto-refresh. Load provider code only after eligibility, consent and performance acceptance.

## 5. Resolve privacy and consent

Document provider processing and the audience-specific consent/CMP requirements. Give usable choices before non-essential software where required. Contextual placement does not automatically remove privacy obligations. Never target using student answers, private reservation data or precise location. A complete ad/CMP integration is not implemented.

## 6. Design direct sponsorship separately

A future fixed-price, clearly labelled placement can fund operations without a transaction cut. Define price, duration, creative rights, prohibited categories, delivery measurement, cancellation/refunds and tax handling before taking payment. Keep organic relevance and safety decisions independent of payment. No sponsored-order/payment workflow or real sponsor inventory exists today.

## 7. Gate Study payments on capacity

Keep BILLING_CHECKOUT_ENABLED=false until merchant onboarding, real tax-inclusive price, test-mode capture/refund/reconciliation, support and capacity acceptance pass. Backend defaults currently cap model calls at 20/day and 100/month; verify actual settings and forecast shared demand before marketing paid limits. These are application defaults, not free-provider allowances or guaranteed papers. Do not raise budgets automatically using existing keys.

Test outages, refunded/revoked membership and duplicate callbacks. Contributions grant no membership or tax-deductible donation claim. Buy Me a Coffee remains unconfigured under the earlier free-only requirement; any future support/payment method needs an owner account and a current fee review.

## 8. Measure sustainability

Budget hosting, DB/egress, tokens/retries, domains, processing fees, moderation and support. Compare actual receipts and costs; CPM/sponsor demand cannot be assumed. Zero commission is a product policy, not a guarantee of zero operating costs. Do not activate paid plans, ads or campaigns merely because the code can render them.
