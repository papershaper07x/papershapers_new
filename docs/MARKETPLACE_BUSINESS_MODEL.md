# Direct local discovery and free reservations

Decision date: 13 October 2026. This is the current pilot model, not a claim of guaranteed savings or a funded operating business.

## The position we can substantiate

**Discover nearby. Deal directly.** Paper Shapers charges no commission on marketplace transactions and no reservation request fee. Browsing and ordinary notice posting are free. Venue prices, taxes, admission fees and offer conditions can still apply; the organiser must state them in the notice. Paper Shapers does not take payment, issue tickets, provide delivery or guarantee attendance, availability or merchant performance.

This is an additional local acquisition channel for independent organisers and businesses. Start in one neighbourhood with a small set of responsive organisers before promising wide coverage. Do not encourage merchants to breach existing contracts, exclusivity or promotional restrictions; they must review their own agreements. Safety screening is not merchant verification. Do not use competitor logos, invented endorsements or unsubstantiated savings claims.

## Attention without deception

Use a short, concrete benefit first, followed immediately by the condition. This uses clarity, local relevance and low perceived risk; conversion impact remains a hypothesis until tested.

| Audience or placement | Copy | Why it fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main heading | Discover nearby. Deal directly. | States the activity and the relationship. |
| Merchant invitation | Your offer. Your earnings. | Emphasises control, paired with the explicit no-platform-cut statement. |
| Visitor invitation | Your next plan starts nearby. | Makes local relevance useful rather than abstract. |
| Fee explanation | 0% Paper Shapers commission. ₹0 reservation request fee. | Names whose fees are zero; does not imply a free meal or event. |
| Reservation button | Reserve — request free | Signals the action and absence of a platform charge. Supporting text states organiser confirmation is required. |
| Interest button | I’m interested | A reversible, low-commitment signal, separate from a booking. |

No fake countdowns, fabricated viewers, seeded popularity, default opt-in, shaming cancellation, disguised ads or guaranteed-best-price claims. India's [CCPA dark-pattern guidance](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1983994&lang=2&noshow=1&reg=3) addresses misleading urgency and disguised advertising, among other practices. Our layout gives cancellation comparable prominence and labels what counts actually measure.

## Reservations and notifications

An organiser opts in while posting and states timing, venue arrangements, charges and conditions. Existing notices default to reservations off. Each signed-in adult request is for one person and requires agreement to share the account display name with that organiser. No email, phone number or attendee list is public. This is an age declaration, not age verification.

States: requested → confirmed or declined; requested/confirmed → cancelled by attendee. The organiser responds only to a pending request. Duplicate submissions cannot add people. Closed requests cannot be reopened repeatedly. Withdrawn, expired or blocked notices refuse new requests and confirmations; visitor history clearly warns that previous confirmation cannot be relied on. Cancellation still works after closure. Availability is managed by the organiser; no capacity, ticket or time-slot guarantee is made.

The organiser inbox and visitor dashboard are the notification surfaces. Updates persist and appear on the next page load/refresh. No background polling, email, SMS, push permission or third-party contact delivery is enabled. If out-of-app alerts are required later, add verified delivery, user preferences, an outbox with idempotency/retries and a budget before changing this promise.

## Honest activity counts

Public cards/details show interested accounts, signed-in detail views, active requests and confirmed requests where applicable. A view records a visible signed-in detail visit, not a card impression or prefetch. Anonymous browsing remains available but is not counted. Each account contributes once; organiser activity is excluded. Interest is reversible. Active requests include confirmed requests and exclude declined/cancelled requests. These are account signals, not verified distinct humans, attendance, sales or endorsements. No extra click counter is presented as a second person. Operators cannot seed displayed metrics through the UI.

## Funding and next decisions

The intended funding is clearly labelled contextual advertisements/sponsorships, separated from ordinary results. No paid sponsorship checkout, ad scripts or revenue claims exist today. Ordinary reach must not depend on buying a sponsorship. Do not use Study answers, attendee identities or precise location for targeting. Ads may cover operating costs; sustainability is unproven and should be measured from real costs rather than assumed.

Pilot measures: real notice supply, organiser response time, requested-to-confirmed ratio and cancellations. Avoid selling account interest data. Run copy tests only once traffic supports a useful sample and do not invent conversion uplift. Prefer nearby/recency relevance to popularity ranking so new local organisers can be discovered.

Remaining: verified merchant onboarding as appropriate, actual event times and optional inventory/party-size workflows, attendance/no-show handling with appeals, paginated historical inbox, out-of-app notifications, retention/deletion operations, ad eligibility and contracts, cloud load testing, moderation operations and sustainable funding. These are explicit future capabilities, not part of the current reservation promise.

## Navigation, discovery and contribution decision
The core Paper Shapers brand returns to the main homepage; portal headers and marketplace footer stay within their portal. State/city/category dropdowns make the discovery path explicit. Locations are curated presets, not rankings or verified coverage. The dummy preview shows fictional listings without bookings or activity counts and remains clearly separate from live notices.

Buy Me a Coffee is not enabled under the free-only requirement: its [official FAQ](https://help.buymeacoffee.com/en/articles/4539170-frequently-asked-questions) specifies a 5% transaction fee, while its [overview](https://help.buymeacoffee.com/en/articles/10182730-what-is-buy-me-a-coffee-and-how-does-it-work) also describes payment-processing charges. Free account setup does not mean fee-free contributions. No account, payout identity, external widget, provider script or charge was created. Existing Study billing is a separate product; it is not linked as marketplace support. A future support method needs an explicit owner/payment address and verified fee terms before activation.
